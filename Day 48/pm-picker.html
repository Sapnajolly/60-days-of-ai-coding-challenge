aggregated = score averaged across multiple review platform ratings, not a single primary number. Pricing reflects annual billing as of mid-2025 — verify at source before purchasing.

Pricing & Free Plan were drawn directly from each tool's official pricing page. ClickUp scores highest on both — its free plan allows unlimited members and its first paid tier runs $7/user/month. Monday.com scores lowest on free plan generosity: its free tier caps at 2 seats, making it effectively unusable for any real team without paying.

Ease of Onboarding is based on G2's "Ease of Setup" sub-score — an aggregated response to the question "How easy was it to set up this product?" from verified reviewers. Asana (8.7) and Monday.com (8.8) consistently top this category. ClickUp (7.7) is frequently described by reviewers as powerful but initially overwhelming.

Feature Depth was assessed by counting distinct view types, task management capabilities, and built-in productivity tools documented on each tool's features page. ClickUp carries the broadest surface area by a clear margin; Asana is the most focused, scoring slightly lower here by design.

Collaboration reflects availability of real-time co-editing, comments, @mentions, proofing tools, and approval workflows. Asana scored highest due to its dedicated proofing and approval features — absent or restricted in other tools at lower plan tiers.

Integrations counts come from each tool's official integrations directory. Raw count was weighted alongside the presence of key connectors (Slack, GitHub, Google Workspace, Zoom) to avoid inflating a score purely on quantity.

Automation reflects both tier availability and engine sophistication. Monday.com and ClickUp offer the most flexible visual builders. Asana's Rules automation is solid. Notion's automation (introduced 2023) remains the most limited — database-scoped, not workflow-wide.

UI Simplicity combines G2's Ease of Use score with recurring qualitative patterns from Capterra reviews. Asana and Monday.com are consistently praised for clean, focused interfaces. ClickUp reviewers frequently flag feature overload. Notion's score reflects its flexibility-vs-complexity tradeoff.

Conflicts resolved: G2 and Capterra scores diverged by 0.2–0.4 points in several cases — the average was used. Integration counts are approximations; vendor directories update frequently.
