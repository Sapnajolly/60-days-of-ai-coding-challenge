What is this?

Your Personal AI Playbook is a workflow system built specifically for Business & Operations professionals who use Claude and ChatGPT for Research & Analysis and Process/SOP work.

The goal: stop re-building prompts from scratch. Instead, use reusable systems that get consistent, high-quality output every time.

The four sections

◈
Workflows — 8 personalized prompt templates with variable slots. Fill in the blanks, copy, and go. Each is a complete prompt system, not a starter sentence.
◎
Prompt Builder — Assemble prompts from modular building blocks: Role, Objective, Context, Reasoning Strategy, Output Format, and more. Every block has an explanation of what it does and why it matters.
↺
Loop Builder — Convert any prompt into an autonomous loop where the AI evaluates and improves its own output until a stop condition is met.
◻
My Library — Prompts you save from anywhere in the app. Searchable. Always accessible. No re-typing.
