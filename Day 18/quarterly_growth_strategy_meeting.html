Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

Transcript mode  ·  Attendees: CEO, Head of Sales, CFO, VP Marketing, Product Director, CTO, Customer Success Lead

Summary

Q2 revenue grew 12%, falling short of the 18% annual target. The gap was driven by enterprise deal slippage (9 of 14 expected contracts closed), lower-than-expected conversion rates despite a 34% increase in website traffic, and a surge in support tickets related to reporting performance. Engineering is near capacity with the August analytics dashboard release. Hiring decisions are postponed pending Q3 forecasts. The annual customer conference budget and analytics dashboard announcement remain pending further reviews.
