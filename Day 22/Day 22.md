# Day 22: Validate Your Startup Idea Like a VC

**Theme:** Entrepreneurship with Claude  
**Focus:** Turn startup assumptions into evidence-based insights

## What I Built
A comprehensive Startup Idea Validation Dashboard that:
- Generates a VC-style scorecard (0–100) across 6 dimensions
- Displays TAM, market growth, and VC interest indicators
- Provides competitive landscape analysis with threat levels
- Maps out a full business model canvas (revenue streams + risks)
- Creates a 90-day go-to-market execution plan

## Startup Idea Validated
**AI-Powered Study Companion for Students** — An AI tutor app that personalizes study plans, generates practice questions, and tracks progress at a fraction of tutoring costs.

### Validation Dimensions
- **Problem Severity**: 85/100 — Real, painful problem for millions of students
- **Market Size**: 90/100 — $8.7B TAM, growing 4.2x over 5 years
- **Competitive Differentiation**: 60/100 — Crowded space, needs strong niche
- **Business Model**: 75/100 — Clear freemium + B2B path
- **GTM Feasibility**: 70/100 — Student communities accessible
- **Team-Market Fit**: 65/100 — Needs subject matter experts

### VC Verdict
Conditional Green Light — strong opportunity but needs a defensible niche to compete.

## Key Learnings
- **Lean Validation Framework**: How VCs assess startups using structured scoring
- **Competitive Moat**: Importance of differentiation in crowded markets
- **Business Model Canvas**: Mapping revenue streams, costs, and risks before building
- **GTM Strategy**: Phased go-to-market approach for early-stage startups
- **Risk Assessment**: Regulatory (COPPA/FERPA), technical (AI hallucinations), and competitive risks

## Claude Prompt Used
*"Act as a Startup Advisor, VC Analyst, and Market Research Expert. Help me validate my startup idea. First ask me: 1. Startup Idea, 2. Target Market, 3. Problem it solves, 4. Current Alternatives..."*
