    ❓ How To Play

    1. Pick a patient scenario below (elective surgery, MRI, specialty medication, or inpatient admission).

    2. Drag the case card forward through each dropzone, left to right: Patient → Provider → Payer.

    3. Some stages require an action first (checking medical necessity, collecting documents) before you can drag the card onward — the dropzone will unlock once the requirement is met.

    4. The Payer issues a determination: Approval, Pend (more info needed), or Denial. A denial can be appealed, and some appeals trigger a Peer-to-Peer Review between doctors.

    5. Every step adds an educational note, may cost simulated days, and affects your Efficiency Score. Try to reach Approval with the fewest days and the highest score!
