# Day 21: Build a Digital Privacy Intelligence Dashboard

**Theme:** Privacy Intelligence with Claude  
**Focus:** Understand digital footprint analysis and create interactive HTML dashboards

## What I Built
A comprehensive Digital Privacy Intelligence Dashboard that:
- Calculates an overall privacy risk score (0–100)
- Analyzes risk levels across 8 major platforms (Facebook, Google, TikTok, etc.)
- Shows 47 exposed data categories with color-coded severity tags
- Displays ecosystem concentration (Google, Meta, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft)
- Provides a prioritized list of 7 actionable privacy recommendations
- Includes a 30-day privacy improvement action plan

## Key Learnings
- **Digital Footprint Analysis**: Understanding what data platforms collect and share
- **Privacy Risk Scoring**: Quantifying exposure across multiple dimensions
- **Platform Ecosystem Concentration**: How using too few platforms increases cross-tracking risk
- **GDPR/CCPA Rights**: How to request data deletion from companies
- **Privacy Tools**: uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, SimpleLogin, Bitwarden, ProtonMail

## Claude Prompt Used
*"Act as a privacy analyst. Analyze this user's digital footprint and create an interactive HTML dashboard showing their privacy risk score, platform-by-platform analysis, and actionable improvement plan..."*

## Tools Used
- Claude AI (claude.ai)
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS
- No external dependencies
